The team

This month's most common question

Worked for two employers? Here's how it affects your refund

Did you work for more than one employer last year – even for a few months? You may be owed a refund. To check, we need Form 106 from each of them.

Read more →

With two employers in the same year, each withholds tax as if it were your only one, unless tax coordination was done. The result is often more tax withheld than needed – one of the most common reasons for a refund.

Form 106 is the annual salary and tax summary every employer issues. Without it we don't have the full picture, and the case stays open.

What to do now:

  1. List every employer you had in the years we're checking – including short or summer jobs.
  2. Ask payroll for the form. Many employers also have it in the employee portal.
  3. Send us a clear photo of each form.

Employer closed, or you've lost touch? Tell us and we'll work out the next step together.

We filed. What happens now?

We filed your claim and it's been quiet since? That's completely normal. Here's the road from here to the money in your account.

Read more →

After filing, the case is reviewed by the tax authority, which sometimes asks for another document. Handling time varies from case to case, and a quiet period doesn't mean something is stuck.

Three things you can do to keep it moving:

  • Make sure the bank details you gave us are current – that's where the refund goes.
  • Got a letter from the tax authority? Snap a photo and send it, even if it looks unimportant.
  • If we ask for a document, send it as soon as you can. Every day it waits is a day the case waits.

On our side: we'll update you on every status change, so you don't have to call and ask.

Donation receipts: money left on the table

Donated to a recognized institution? That receipt is worth money. Many people keep it in a drawer and forget to send it.

Read more →

In Israel, a donation to a public institution recognized under Section 46 of the Income Tax Ordinance earns a tax credit of 35% of the donation, subject to conditions and a minimum annual amount.

How to check a receipt qualifies:

  • It states the institution is recognized under Section 46.
  • It's in your name, or your spouse's.
  • Digital receipts sent by email count too.

Gather receipts from the years we're checking and send them over, even if you're not sure. We'll check which ones qualify.
