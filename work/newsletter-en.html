Worked for two employers? Here's how it affects your refund

With two employers in the same year and no tax coordination, more tax than needed is often withheld. It's one of the most common reasons for a refund.

To calculate it, we need Form 106 from every employer – even a short or summer job.

  • Don't have the form? Ask the payroll department.
  • Employer no longer exists? Tell us and we'll work out the next step together.

We filed. Now what?

After filing, the case is with the tax authority, and handling time varies. The quiet doesn't mean something is stuck.

What you can do: make sure we have your current bank details, and reply quickly if we ask for another document. We'll update you on every status change.

Donation receipts: money left on the table

Donations to a recognized institution earn a tax credit. Many people keep the receipts and forget to send them.

Gather receipts from recent years and send them over. We'll check which qualify and include them.
