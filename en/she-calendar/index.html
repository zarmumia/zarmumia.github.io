How it works

1
Pick what you do
Tick only what's relevant to you. Even one thing is enough.
2
Tell it when you last did it
And how often you like to do it. Sensible defaults are already filled in.
3
Get reminded in time
The next one appears in your calendar, with a reminder early enough to book.

Made with respect for your privacy

  • Works only inside its own calendar, "She Calendar". It never sees your other events.
  • Your list stays in your own Google account. No outside servers, no ads, nothing sold.
  • In English, and in Hebrew for Hebrew-language accounts.
