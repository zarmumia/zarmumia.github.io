How it works

1
Pick what you do
Tick only what's relevant to you. Even one thing is enough.
2
Tell it when you last did it
And how often you like to do it. Sensible defaults are already filled in.
3
Get reminded in time
The next one appears in your calendar, with a reminder early enough to book.

What's inside the Android app

"Done" in one tapThe next date is counted from the day you actually went.
Reminders with buttonsDone, Snooze a week, or Call, right from the notification.
Your treatments in one placeWhen you last went, when's next, who does it, a note. "Find nearby" opens Google Maps with the search ready.
Recommended checkupsFor your age and country, from official health sources (Ministry of Health, USPSTF, NHS), each with a link.
"Your year"A heart fills up every time you do something for yourself. And small celebrations on the days that are yours, like your birthday.
Period reminders, only if you wantOff by default, with an event name you choose.

Recommended checkups are general information, not medical advice. Please consult your doctor. For adults, 18 and over.

Made with respect for your privacy

  • In the Android app: everything stays on your phone. No account with us, no server, no tracking. Reminders are saved as private events in your own calendar.
  • In the calendar add-on: it works only inside its own calendar, "She Calendar", and never sees your other events. Your list stays in your own Google account.
  • No ads, nothing sold. We never ask for a diagnosis, only what you do and when.
  • In English and Hebrew.
