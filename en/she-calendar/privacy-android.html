She Calendar for Android — Privacy Policy

A product by Zarmumia

Effective date: November 12, 2026

She Calendar helps you keep track of recurring self-care: beauty treatments, health checkups and appointments. It reminds you on time and writes the next date into your own calendar. This policy explains what the Android app does with your information. (The Google Calendar add-on has its own policy.)

In short: your information stays on your phone. The app has no server, no account with us, no analytics, no ads and no internet access of its own. We (Zarmumia) never receive your information.

What is saved, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone, inside the app:

  • Your treatments and checkups: name, how often, the dates you did them, and, if you choose to add them, who does it, their phone number, address and a note.
  • A little about you: age group, country, life stage and whether your doctor recommended a different schedule. Used only to show checkups that fit you.
  • Your birthday (day and month only, no year), if you choose to add it.
  • Period dates, only if you turn this optional feature on (it is off by default).
  • Your settings.

This information is not backed up to any cloud and is not copied to a new phone. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted. We never ask for a diagnosis or a medical condition.

Your calendar

With your permission, the app writes reminders as private events into the calendar you choose on your phone (usually your Google Calendar). Your calendar provider (for example Google) syncs those events under its own privacy policy. The app reads only the list of your calendars (to let you choose one) and the events it created itself. It does not read your other events.

Inviting friends to your celebration

If you use "Invite friends", you pick friends one at a time with your phone's own contact picker. The app sees only the email address of the contact you picked, not your other contacts. The invitation is created as an event in your calendar with your friends as guests, and your calendar provider sends the invitations. Guests do not see each other's addresses. We keep nothing.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The lock screen shows no details unless you allow it.

Things that happen only when you tap

  • Sharing a picture (for example "Your year") opens your phone's share menu; it goes only to the app and people you choose.
  • "Call", "Take me there", "Find nearby" and "Order on Wolt" open your phone's dialer, maps, Waze or Wolt app. Only the phone number, address or search words are passed to that app. We have no agreement with Wolt and receive nothing from it.
  • Links to official health sources open in your browser.

Health information

Recommended checkups come from official sources (such as the Israeli Ministry of Health, USPSTF and the NHS), with a link to the source. They are general information, not medical advice. Please consult your doctor.

Deleting your information

Settings → "Delete all my information" deletes everything the app saved and the events it created in your calendar. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything saved on the phone.

Age

She Calendar is intended for adults, 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
