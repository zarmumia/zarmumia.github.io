The lead's journey

This is how a lead moves through your business today.
And how it could.

Five stops between "I left my details" and "a client who refers others". At each one, money can disappear – or arrive.

New inquiry

The inquiry lands in someone’s email or WhatsApp – and gets forgotten.Every inquiry flows into the CRM automatically and is assigned to a rep.

First response

"We’ll call you tomorrow" – and nobody does.An automatic reminder, and an alert to the manager when a lead goes unanswered.

Call & notes

What was said and what’s next lives only in the rep’s head.An AI call summary is saved to the record, with the next step.

Closing

No clear picture: how many are in the pipeline, where they’re stuck, how much money.A CEO dashboard: the funnel, the bottlenecks and the money – on one page.

A client who refers

Months of silence, and "what’s happening with my case?" calls.Automatic status updates, and a referral request at the right moment.

Every one of these stops is money walking out the door.And suddenly, everything works.

The story

Zarmumia knows how to renew itself.

What is a zarmumia? In Moroccan culture, the zarmumia is a small lizard – the house gecko – that brings luck. When it appears on the wall, it's a good sign: the home is protected and blessed.

That's exactly how we work: we come in quietly, don't take up space, notice the small details – and suddenly, everything works.

Founded by Fanny Ben Ami Rabinovitch – former VP Service & Operations and co-founder of a payments startup. Works with clients worldwide, remotely.

First incarnation

Strategic marketing

Zarmumia began as a marketing studio (Sharp Marketing): strategy, campaigns, content and branding. That's where we learned that good marketing brings leads – but if a lead isn't answered, documented and closed, the money is lost along the way.

Today

From marketing to operations

Today Zarmumia connects marketing and operations: diagnosis, automation, dashboards and hands-on support – so every lead makes it all the way.

Privacy from day one

  • Minimal identifying data leaves your systems
  • We work with our own user account – never your password
  • Every automation is checked against applicable privacy regulations
